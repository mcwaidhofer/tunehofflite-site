TunehoffLite Privacy Policy

Effective October 5, 2026

TunehoffLite is a guitar tuner. Its privacy policy is short because it collects nothing.

Microphone

TunehoffLite uses your device's microphone for one purpose: detecting the pitch of the string you are playing, live, on your device. Audio is processed in memory and immediately discarded. It is never recorded, stored, or transmitted anywhere. If you deny microphone access, the tuner simply cannot hear you — nothing else changes.

Data collection

TunehoffLite collects no data. No analytics, no identifiers, no tracking, no advertising SDKs, no accounts, and no network requests. Your settings (tuning, skin, reference pitch) are stored only on your device.

Third parties

TunehoffLite shares nothing with third parties, because it has nothing to share.

Children

TunehoffLite is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the change will be listed here with a new effective date before it ships.
