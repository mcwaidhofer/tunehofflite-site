TunehoffLite Support

The tuner doesn't react when I play

TunehoffLite needs microphone access. Open Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone and turn on TunehoffLite. The status pill at the bottom of the screen shows Mic access off or No microphone when it can't hear you.

How do I read it?

The big letter is the note the string should be. The number is how far off you are in cents: − means flat (tune up), + means sharp (tune down). The top of the screen says Tune up, Tune down or In tune.

How do I change tuning or skin?

Tap the gear icon in the top-right corner.

Tuning one specific string

Tap a string button (E A D G B e) to lock the tuner onto that string. Tap it again to go back to automatic detection.
